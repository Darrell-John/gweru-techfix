About Gweru TechFix

A student-led initiative from Midlands State University to solve Gweru's tech problems.

Our Story

Started in 2026 by 6 IT students who saw expensive repair costs in Mkoba, Senga and CBD. We offer affordable, honest, and fast service.

Our Mission

To provide reliable laptop, phone and software repairs while selling affordable accessories to students and local businesses.

Our Location

Main Road, near MSU, Gweru. We also do pick-up and delivery in Mkoba 6, 13, 18 and Senga.
